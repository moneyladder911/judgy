# Daily TikTok / Reels / Shorts scripts

One format, every day: show the thing, type it into the site, reveal the card. 15 to 40 seconds. Screen-record the site on your phone; your face in the corner is optional but helps.

**Every video:**

1. **Hook (first 2 seconds):** the product on screen + the line in big text.
2. **Type it in:** show the purchase being typed. Include the price.
3. **Judges deliberate:** let the five judges animate (it's the suspense).
4. **Reveal:** pause on the stamp. Read the score and the #1 red flag out loud.
5. **Close:** "Comment what you're about to buy and I'll run it next." Link in bio.

**Rules:** use real products people are actually seeing that week (check TikTok Shop, Amazon movers, viral ads). Never claim a specific product or company is a scam; say what the panel flagged. Run each one through the real panel on the day, so the verdict you show is the one the site gave. Post 1 to 3 a day, same time, same look.
