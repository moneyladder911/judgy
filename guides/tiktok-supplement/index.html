Should I buy a supplement I saw on TikTok?

Walk away 3/10

Be very careful. Supplements don't have to prove they work before they're sold, and creator videos are usually paid ads.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Before-and-after videos and testimonials aren't evidence.
  2. "Subscribe and save" can quietly turn into monthly charges.
  3. Some supplements interact with medicines. Check with a pharmacist or doctor first.

Cheaper or safer instead

Ask a pharmacist what has real evidence for your goal. It's often a cheap, plain product.

Before you pay

  • Look for independent third-party testing on the label.
  • Check if the creator is sponsored.
  • Read the cancellation terms before subscribing.
