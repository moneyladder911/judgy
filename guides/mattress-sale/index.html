Should I buy a mattress on a "today only" sale?

Wait 5/10

Mattress sales run most of the year, so "today only" rarely is. If you need a new mattress, buy one with a long home trial instead of chasing the deadline.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. The list price is often set high so the sale price looks like a bargain.
  2. Returns can be expensive or have pick-up fees. Check before you buy.
  3. Showroom tests of a few minutes tell you little about sleeping on it for months.

Cheaper or safer instead

A well-reviewed online mattress with a 100-night trial and free returns.

Before you pay

  • Find out how long the trial is and who pays for a return.
  • Check the price history if the store lets you.
  • Read the 1-star reviews for sagging and durability complaints.
