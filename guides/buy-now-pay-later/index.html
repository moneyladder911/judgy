Should I use buy now, pay later?

Wait 4.5/10

It's fine for something you could pay for today anyway. It's a problem when it's the only way you can afford it, because the payments stack up.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Several small plans at once add up fast and are easy to lose track of.
  2. Missed payments can bring late fees and may affect your credit.
  3. Splitting the price makes impulse buys feel cheaper than they are.

Cheaper or safer instead

Wait until you can pay in full. If it's still worth it after saving for a few weeks, buy it then.

Before you pay

  • Add up every plan you already have running.
  • Read the late fee terms.
  • Ask: would I buy this if I had to pay all of it today?
