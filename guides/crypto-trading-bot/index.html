Should I buy a crypto trading bot?

Walk away 1/10

No. Bots and signal groups promising steady daily returns match the most common investment scam pattern. Nobody sells a machine that prints money.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Guaranteed or steady daily returns are the classic sign of a scam.
  2. Many of these platforms let money in but make withdrawals difficult or impossible.
  3. If you're asked to recruit friends or deposit more to unlock profits, walk away.

Cheaper or safer instead

If you want to invest, a low-cost, regulated index fund. If you're already in, try a small withdrawal now and report the platform to your regulator if it fails.

Before you pay

  • Search your financial regulator's warning list for the company name.
  • Never send money to someone you met through a DM.
  • Talk to a qualified financial adviser before investing large sums.
