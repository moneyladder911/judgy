Should I buy the extended warranty?

Walk away 3/10

Usually no. Extended warranties are priced so the store makes money on them, and most products don't break in the extra years they cover.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Many credit cards already add an extra year of warranty to purchases.
  2. The fine print often excludes the things that actually break, like batteries and accidental damage.
  3. The sales push at the till is a sign of how profitable these plans are.

Cheaper or safer instead

Put the warranty money in a savings pot for repairs. If you are clumsy with phones, compare the maker's own accident plan instead of the store's.

Before you pay

  • Check what the manufacturer's warranty already covers.
  • Check your credit card's purchase protection.
  • Read exactly what is excluded before deciding.
