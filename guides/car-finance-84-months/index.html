Should I finance a car over 84 months?

Walk away 3/10

Usually no. A seven-year loan lowers the monthly payment but raises the total you pay, and you can owe more than the car is worth for years.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Longer loans usually mean more interest in total.
  2. You may be "underwater" (owing more than it's worth) for much of the loan.
  3. The low monthly figure can push you into a more expensive car than you need.

Cheaper or safer instead

A cheaper car over a shorter loan, with financing arranged through your own bank or credit union before visiting the dealer.

Before you pay

  • Ask for the total cost of the loan, all payments added up.
  • Get a quote from your own bank first.
  • Compare the same car over 48 or 60 months.
