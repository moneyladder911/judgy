Should I buy an online trading course?

Walk away 2.5/10

Usually not. Most paid trading courses sell the dream of quitting your job, and the income screenshots come from the few who got lucky.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Income screenshots and lifestyle photos are marketing, not proof that students make money.
  2. Countdown timers and "only 3 spots left" are pressure tactics. Real education doesn't expire tonight.
  3. Many courses upsell signals groups, coaching or a "pro" tier once you're in.

Cheaper or safer instead

Learn the basics free from your broker's education pages, library books and regulator guides, and practise on a demo account for three months before spending anything.

Before you pay

  • Ask for audited results of typical students, not the best ones.
  • Get the refund policy in writing before you pay.
  • Search the course name plus "scam", "refund" and "reddit".
