Should I buy the new iPhone or last year's model?

Wait 6/10

If your phone works, last year's model or a refurbished one does nearly everything for a lot less. Buy new only if a specific new feature matters to you.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Year-to-year upgrades are usually small. Check what's actually different for how you use it.
  2. 0% financing makes the price feel smaller but locks you in for years.
  3. Trade-in offers often pay less than selling your old phone yourself.

Cheaper or safer instead

A manufacturer-refurbished or last-generation model with a full warranty, often 20 to 30% cheaper.

Before you pay

  • List the two features you'd actually use every day.
  • Compare the refurbished price from the maker's own store.
  • Price your old phone on a resale site before accepting a trade-in.
