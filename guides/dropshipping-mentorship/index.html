Should I buy a dropshipping mentorship?

Walk away 2/10

Usually not. The person selling the mentorship often makes more from selling mentorships than from dropshipping.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Revenue screenshots show sales, not profit after ads, refunds and fees.
  2. Much of the material is available free on YouTube and blogs.
  3. High-ticket coaching often comes with pressure to pay for more tiers.

Cheaper or safer instead

Spend a month on free material and test one product with a small ad budget you can afford to lose. You'll learn more for far less.

Before you pay

  • Ask for proof of profit, not revenue.
  • Get the refund policy in writing.
  • Find past students who aren't affiliates.
