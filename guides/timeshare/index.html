Should I buy a timeshare?

Walk away 1.5/10

Almost always no. Timeshares come with yearly fees that tend to rise, they're hard to resell, and the sales presentations are built around pressure.

The panel’s general take. Your exact price, seller and situation can change the verdict, so check yours below.

Top 3 red flags

  1. Annual maintenance fees continue for years and often go up.
  2. Resale values are typically far below what buyers paid.
  3. "Free" gifts for attending a presentation are bait for a high-pressure pitch.

Cheaper or safer instead

Book holiday rentals as you go. You keep your money and your freedom to go somewhere else next year.

Before you pay

  • Check the cooling-off period where you are, and use it if you've signed.
  • Look up resale listings for the same resort.
  • Never sign on the day of the presentation.
