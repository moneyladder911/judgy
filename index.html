      FAQ

      Good questions. Ask them before you buy, too.

      Why not just ask a chatbot?

      You can. A single chatbot tends to agree with how you framed the question. Our panel is five judges set up to disagree with each other, and it always ends in the same clear format: a verdict, a score, three red flags and an alternative. It takes a minute, and the card is made to share.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. A verdict is a second opinion to help you think, not professional financial, legal or medical advice. For big decisions, talk to a qualified professional.

      Do sellers pay you for good verdicts?

      Never. No seller can buy, sponsor or influence a verdict. When the cheaper alternative is a product, we may link to a store search with an affiliate tag and label it. That commission never changes a verdict, and it’s never on the thing you asked about.

      How does the 30-day pass work?

      You pay once and get unlimited checks for 30 days. It doesn’t renew on its own, so there’s nothing to cancel. Payments are handled securely by Ziina and may be charged in UAE dirhams (AED).

      What can I check?

      Anything you’re about to spend real money on: cars, courses, gadgets, furniture, memberships, “investment opportunities”, even that subscription you’re not sure about.

      What happens to what I type?

      It’s used to write your verdict. We don’t sell it, and we won’t share a card unless you do.
